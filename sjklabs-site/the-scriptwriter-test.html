Start here when the story feels wrong

You can feel the drag. The sales calls explain the business better than the site does. The market is seeing something flatter than the truth.

Who this is for

  • A founder who knows the business is stronger than the way it currently sounds.
  • A CMO or commercial lead trying to work out whether the problem is positioning, urgency or language.
  • A leadership team about to brief copy, PR or a new homepage and wanting the diagnosis before the output.

What you get

Test 01

Protagonist

Is the story about the right person? We identify whether your business or your customer is at the centre — and what that costs you commercially.

Test 02

Stakes

Is the cost of inaction clear and urgent? We assess whether the story gives buyers a reason to act now or lets them walk away unconvinced.

Test 03

Dialogue

Does it sound human and repeatable? We read the actual language in use and name where it becomes jargon, brochure, or noise.

What this is not

  • Not a full website production project — draft copy is included, but if you want the pages taken to finished and publish-ready, that's Extended Copy Work (available as an add-on).
  • Not a content plan or campaign brief.
  • Not a brand workshop with fifteen stakeholders in a room.
  • Not a substitute for the wider retrieval audit if the problem extends beyond the story itself.

The Scriptwriter Test™ includes draft page copy as standard. Extended Copy Work is for clients who want that draft developed into finished, publish-ready pages — refined, structured, and ready to hand to a designer or go live. Scope and cost are agreed per project. Book a call to discuss.
